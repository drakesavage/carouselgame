# Trap records (approved 2026-10-25; applied as correction #13)

target area, Check, Difficulty, damage and effects, reset procedure, bulk and condition before it is deployed. None of
the party's traps has ever had these recorded (they arrived from DCC-DENTON-009 as names and "ready"), so the console
refuses to deploy them. The owner approved the records below as written; they were applied as correction #13 (save point #25) and are locked while a trap is deployed.

**What is from the record and what is proposed.** The Murder Drum Battery's Skyhook Harpoon and Kill-Lane Wings are
copied from `state/shared_inventory.yaml` (their build records). Everything else is proposed, anchored on the books:
the Floor 3 Unopposed Difficulty (10 + 2 × Floor = **16**); a trap's attack at **+10** (a trained crawler's Floor 3
attack; the builds' totals were never recorded); damage dice from the published weapons they imitate (Crossbow 1d8;
